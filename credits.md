# Credits

## Inspiration and ideas

- **[UnclePunch](https://github.com/UnclePunch)**: The one, the only. His training tools were the main inspiration for this tool, as well as some concrete references: how we use the event page takeover, and how the training tools give feedback such as tips that the player was late.
- **[Alex Harrison (Aitch)](https://github.com/AlexanderHarrison)**: Maintainer of [Training Mode - Community Edition](https://github.com/AlexanderHarrison/TrainingMode-CommunityEdition), whose release model we follow: players apply a patch to their own vanilla ISO rather than downloading the game. We also use their [gc_fst](https://github.com/AlexanderHarrison/gc_fst) tool to rebuild the disc image.
- **[Kadence](https://www.youtube.com/@KadencePlays)**: For sharing the idea of making the opponent invisible after the first 5 frames.
- **[PhiDX](https://www.youtube.com/@PhiDX)**: For the basis of this curriculum, which is based on his videos teaching players how to learn throw-breaking in Tekken.
- **[AI Hero](https://www.aihero.dev/)**: I used Matt Pocock's recommended AI workflow to build the tool.

## Disclosure

I didn't hand-code anything. All of the code was written using a Claude AI subscription.
